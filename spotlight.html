The idea

One Saturday. One business. Your people show up.

Spotlight Saturdays exists to amplify community talent and help local businesses grow. Each Saturday, featured businesses get a space in the studio, the promotion, and the crowd.

We spotlight a business on select Saturdays from October 31 through December 19, so every feature gets real attention.

If you sell a product, offer a service, or run something you built from nothing, this is your stage.

  • Your own spaceA dedicated space inside the studio to show what you do.
  • Promotion before and afterYour business gets featured across GorJess Studios channels.
  • Face to face with customersMeet new people in person and leave with real connections.

Questions

Before you sign up

Who can be featured?

Any local business, product maker, or service provider. If you are building something in the community, apply.

Do I need to be a beauty business?

No. GorJess Studios is a beauty space, but Spotlight Saturdays is open to all kinds of local businesses.

What happens after I sign up?

We contact you to confirm your Saturday and answer any questions. Nothing is final until we confirm with you.

Which Saturdays are available?

October 31, November 14, November 28, December 12, and December 19.

Can I pick a different date later?

Yes. Just let us know and we will work with you.
