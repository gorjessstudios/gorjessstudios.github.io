Frequently Asked Questions

Tap a question to see the answer. Still have one that is not here? Ask us when we follow up about your tour.

What is a booth or suite rental?

It is a professional space where you run your own beauty business, keep your own clients, and set your own prices, without the overhead of opening an entire salon.

How does leasing work?

1. Complete this pre-lease application. 2. We review it and reach out within 48 hours to set up a tour. 3. Tour the studio and decide on a booth or suite. 4. Sign your rental agreement and pay your deposit. 5. Move in.

How long is the lease?

We offer 6 and 12-month leases, billed weekly.

What does the space cost?

Booths and suites are the same price. The 12-month lease is $150 per week and the 6-month lease is $165 per week. Rent is billed weekly and auto-drafted every Saturday by 7:00 PM through Square.

What perks do I get?

Every new approved renter gets the first week for $100. The 12-month lease also locks your rate for the full term and earns 4 free weeks: 1 free week at months 3, 6, 9, and 12. The 6-month lease does not include the free weeks.

What does my rent cover?

Your rent covers your space and the building's utilities. Laundry is not included.

How often is rent due?

Weekly. Rent is auto-drafted through Square every Saturday by 7:00 PM.

What is the difference between a booth and a suite?

Booths are on the open floor and come furnished with a chair, station, and mirror. Suites are unfurnished, and a chair is available on request. Suites have walls but are not fully enclosed or soundproof.

What do I bring?

Your own products, tools, and clients. We provide the space, and the chair, station, and mirror in booths.

Do you get a lot of walk-in traffic?

GorJess Studios is made for beauty professionals who already have clientele, or who have the work ethic to build it. We are not a walk-in heavy space, and we will not promise you bookings. We promote our directory and run community events like Spotlight Saturdays, but your clients are your own.

Is there parking?

Yes. Free parking is available in the lot out front.

What are the studio hours?

Weekly renters have access from 7am to 11pm, so you can work around your own schedule and your clients' schedules.

Do I need insurance?

Yes. All renters are required to carry liability insurance.

Do I need a license?

If your service requires one, we will ask for a copy or proof before you move in. Liability insurance is also required.

What is the deposit?

A $150 security deposit is due at signing and is refundable per the rental agreement.

What if I need to leave early?

Either party can end the agreement with 30 days' written notice. Leaving without 30 days' notice means a $150 early termination fee and a forfeited deposit.

Can I try the space first?

Yes. After your application is reviewed, we will set up a tour and can talk about a trial day.
