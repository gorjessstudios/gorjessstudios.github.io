Good to know
How does leasing work?

1. Complete this pre-lease application. 2. We review it and reach out within 48 hours to set up a tour. 3. Tour the studio and decide on a booth or suite. 4. Sign your rental agreement and pay your deposit. 5. Move in.

What does the space cost?

Booths and suites are the same price. The 12-month lease is $150 per week and the 6-month lease is $165 per week. Rent is billed weekly and auto-drafted every Saturday by 7:00 PM through Square.

What perks do I get?

Every new approved renter gets the first week for $100. The 12-month lease also locks your rate for the full term and earns 4 free weeks: 1 free week at months 3, 6, 9, and 12. The 6-month lease does not include the free weeks.

What is included in my rent?

Your rent includes your booth or suite, a key to your suite, maintenance of common areas, wifi, electricity, water, heating and cooling, the waiting area, your directory listing, and the security camera system. Booths come fully furnished. Suites are unfurnished, and a chair is available on request. Renters are responsible for cleaning their own space. Laundry is not included.

Do you get a lot of walk-in traffic?

GorJess Studios is made for beauty professionals who already have clientele, or who are fully committed to building it. We are not a walk-in heavy space, and we will not promise you bookings. Success here comes from your own consistency, marketing, and work ethic. We promote our directory and run community events like Spotlight Saturdays, but your clients are your own. If you just want to test the waters, a daily rental is a better fit than a lease.

Is there parking?

Yes. Free parking is available in the lot out front.

Do I need insurance?

Yes. All renters are required to carry liability insurance.
