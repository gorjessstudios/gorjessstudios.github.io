How It Works

Book it, work it, leave it clean.

01
Book your day

Pick a space and a date, answer the short intake form and pay at booking.

02
Show up and work

Your space is yours from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Bring your clients, your products and your tools.

03
Clean up and go

Wipe down, sanitize and take out your trash. Take all your things with you.

Before You Book

What a daily rental means.

Bring your own supplies

Products, tools and anything else you use for your services come with you.

Your clients, your schedule

You book and collect from your own clients. The studio is mostly appointment based, so plan to bring your own.

Leave it clean

Nothing stays at the station after your day ends. Sanitize and clean up before you leave.

Licensed where required

You will enter your license information when you book.
